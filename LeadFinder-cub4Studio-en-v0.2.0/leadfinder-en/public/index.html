GET STARTED WITH CLARITY

Before your
next search.

Do I need to set up an API key?

No. Subscribe to a plan and use integrated search inside Lead Finder. No external provider account is needed.

How does the allowance work?

Your plan allows 100, 300 or 600 requested business records per paid cycle. Each search accepts up to 25. Requests sent to the provider reserve the requested limit, including searches with fewer results or a failure, to avoid repeated charges. Confirmed renewal starts a new cycle.

Where is my data saved?

Businesses, pipeline, activities and tasks are saved in your account on the server. Sign in on another device using the same account. You can also export your CRM to CSV.

Does the system send messages?

Log contacts, prepare a draft and manage next steps. Messages and external notifications are not sent automatically.

Are these prospects ready to buy?

Search returns public information when available. You verify the data and qualify the opportunity. Email enrichment and WhatsApp validation are not included.

Can I use a Maps search link?

The Maps shortcut opens a manual search without a key. It does not import results. Use integrated search to bring businesses into the CRM automatically.
