A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your
next search.

Can I subscribe to Lead Finder now?

The product page and workspace are available. Subscriptions will open after customer accounts, billing and per-customer credits are implemented. Contact cub4Studio about the launch.

Are these contacts ready to buy?

No. These are business details to support prospecting. Qualification, data verification and outreach are part of your sales process.

Does the product find emails and WhatsApp?

The current integration retrieves business information, including public phone numbers where available. It does not enrich email addresses or verify WhatsApp availability.

Where are my lists saved?

In this version, lists are saved in the browser you use. Export a CSV to keep a copy. Lists are not synced between devices.

Does the system send messages automatically?

No. You can edit and copy an outreach draft. You decide when and how to contact each business.
